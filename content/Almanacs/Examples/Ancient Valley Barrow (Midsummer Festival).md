An ancient Barrrow, populated by Mouras Encantada, Cuelebres, or something else. Protecting Burial goods and a portal (only active in the ethereal realm, leads to a shadowfell staircase to Mourama (shadowfell underdark?)) to the Shadowfell. Dungeon overtaken by green slime(100 exp per character un-slimed split)

## Hoards
Mouras 6 Cr total(individuals spread across)
Culelebre 6 Cr total (on midsummers night) (hoardx2)8400 gold 3 magic items
# Getting there
Rappel or convince someone in the roost to take you.
# Entrance
(random encounter table of Mouras?) 3 2CR (1250exp) Enchanted Mouras large constructs wield with distaff spears
# Puzzle
Gallery room, with a relief in the wall of Mouros goldsmithing, and Mouras building the barrows above them. Puzzle opens hidden door to room with extra treasure(and 1000 exp split), or on fail to a trap(locks behind them, then floods)
# Trick
robing room if vested properly can turn ethereal inside of the barrow, guides not to hard to find but trick items unnecessary to rituals useful to the player complicate the process
Glyphs that when pressed, display illusory images of how to properly wear the vestements
2 Candlesticks that float away from anyone that reaches for them, if 2 dif people grab them swaps their minds then become inert
Obelisk that ages first person to touch it
# Boss
Cuelebre (2400exp) fight, near a sort of balcony in the barrows, lower and towards the sea hidden by an overhang and rough ocean, shrine to a sort of cthonic mountain/guide god, seemingly protecting the barrow from erosion (statue of it as a winged mountain with an eye) (maybe a serpentine woman instead?)
# Reward
Tomb of an ancient king in front of an innactive, shadowfell portal but active in the ethereal realm(1000exp split if portal discovered active)
