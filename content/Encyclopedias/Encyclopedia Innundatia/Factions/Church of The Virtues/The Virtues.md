The gods of the [[Septean]] people, each the embodiement of value important to them.
**Temperance**
Called "The Quartermaster of the Gods." Appearing as a broad, powerful, charming man with a metalic eye with a stellar blue pupil. (eye of Shorja is the name of a star)
**Patience**
The Virtue of Patience is the patron of fishers and hunters. Buccaneers originally referring to landless hunters, and eventually confering the meaning of the name onto pirates, has caused him to be favored by, if not the unofficial patron of pirates.
**Kindness**
The Virtues Navigator Patron of The [[The League of Stars]]
**Humility**
The Captain of [[The Virtues]] crew. The way he appears, one would probably not assume the small unassuming Halfling in simple sailors garb to be a captain, let alone chief of a pantheon of gods.
**Dilligence**
Coxswain of the Virtues
**Chastity**
Represents the isolation of sailing as well as the virtue of chastity. Appears as a young person, alone in a crows nest and no mater how hard you try they're always looming above you at a distance.
**Charity**
Virtue of Charity and cook of the gods.
