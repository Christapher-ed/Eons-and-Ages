The gods of the [[Septean]] people, each the embodiement of value important to them. But the full spectrum of that values, and opposing sin/taboo the full spectrum
## ~~Temperance~~ The Quartermaster Magnificence
The Quartermaster of The Absolute Appearing as a broad, powerful, charming man with a metalic eye with a stellar blue pupil. (eye of Shorja is the name of a star)
## ~~Patience~~ The Buccaneer Tempered
The Virtue of Patience is the patron of fishers and hunters. Buccaneers originally referring to landless hunters, and eventually confering the meaning of the name onto pirates, has caused him to be favored by, if not the unofficial patron of pirates.
## ~~Kindness~~ The Navigator Indignation
The Virtues Navigator Patron of The [[The League of the Stars]]
## ~~Humility~~ The Captain Magnanimity
The Captain of [[The Crew of The Absolute]] . The way he appears, one would probably not assume the small unassuming Halfling in simple sailors garb to be a captain, let alone chief of a pantheon of gods.
## ~~Dilligence~~ The Coxswain Dilligence
Coxswain of the Virtues
## ~~Chastity~~ The Lookout Modesty
Represents the isolation of sailing as well as modesty. Appears as a young person, alone in a crows nest and no mater how hard you try they're always looming above you at a distance. Associated with Crows, mail, and The Morrigan (they are pen pals)
## ~~Charity~~ The Cook Liberality
Virtue of Charity and cook of the gods.
