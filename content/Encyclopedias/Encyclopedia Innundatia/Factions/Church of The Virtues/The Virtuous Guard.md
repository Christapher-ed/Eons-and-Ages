One of the most well equipped forces in Inundatia, using glowing gold muskets and plater armor. These warriors answer only to the Archbishop of Septea. More and more these former guards of pilgrims and holy places are becoming [[Prince-Archbishop Maximus IX]]'s personal enforcers. 

7 orders of 7 groups of 100

Grand Marshall leads the Guard, appointed by the Prince-Archbishop

Each of the 7 orders has a Knight Commander

each 100 split into 14 groups of 7 plus a Captain and a chaplain

each group of 7, 5 halberdiers a Sergeant and a cleric

Occasionally reinforced by men-at-arms of the church, or in later years Septean Skyguard
