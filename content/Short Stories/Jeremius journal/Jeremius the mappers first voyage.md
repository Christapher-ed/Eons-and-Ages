I came from a large family in the capital city of Septea Cyodue, which is useful in The Seven Cities, but only if they're part of the church or a guild. We were all laborers and peasants though a great uncle of mine was an apprentice in one of the first guilds, but he never got any further so that was our peak. When I got old enough to work the best my family could swing me is life as a dockworker, was fine enough for awhile but I've always been one to get tired of seeing the same things.

I musta been in my late teens, two years of lifting boxes on and off ships, and I decided I wanted to see something new. Next time I was alone in a ship bound for Jaysos I hid in an empty barrel, and waited for as long as I could. Eventually I fell asleep, and didn't wake up til I'd been moved to shore and someone began pouring water in to which I spat and got out. 

I was in a different city marshy and along a river, this must have been Jaysos, I was also surrounded by burly sailors and a couple merchant looking fellas. I shrugged and said, "Uh greetings," to which one merchant shook and chastised me for ruining his goods, and other laughed saying, "I'll still pay you, that laugh was plenty worth it," a couple of the sailors held me by the shoulders as the merchants finished their buisness, then the one who chuckled and whose ship I was appearently on turned to me, and had his sailors let go of me, "Now son, why were you hiding in one of my barrels." I shook nervously as I explained to him my life story and dreams of seeing distant lands, rediscovering places and cultures lost to or changed by the flood, the more I told him the more annoyed with me and more amused by me he seemed to get til he clapped me on the back and guided me towards his ship, "Tell ye what son, we can take on one more crew member but ye'll have to do yer part, maybe even learn a bit of a trade from one a me officers" 

I was introduced to all the officers and taught the basics of what I'd have to do, for now it'd mostly be cleaning and loading and unloading boxes which I suppose I was already proficient in. We left the busy bog town of Jaysos the Cathedral of Charity overlooking the Geyser and sprawling town around both. The ship was filled to the brim with water, colonists, and food to take to the Prince-Bishoprics colony of Royllo. I took a moment to admire all the wetland around the winding river we went through, between cleaning duties and being shoved between passengers, I'd only read about places like this in the churches library seeing the world sure is something else.

A little over a day later, we'd gotten out of the river, and Royllo was on the horizon a few hours after that. When we docked at the small but busy port colonists rushing off to new lives and new fortunes as we unloaded food, and water. The Captain said we'd be here a few days while he aquired some things gave us a bit of pay and went off with his quarter master up into the wetland Frogdog farms. 

I took the opprotunity to explore this new city, so far was not to different from Cyodue but much smaller, I stopped by the church, it looked a lot like the one in Jaysos and the services were a lot nicer than those in Cyodue, as I left they were handing out bread and I took some which was suprisingly delicious for plain bread appearently baked with Royllo frogdog eggs. 

After hearing a lot about these frog dogs I thought I'd check them out, I knew the Captain and Quartermaster were looking at them right now so maybe I could catch up with them. After following a country road up the cliffs and into the ranches, I looked out at the frogdogs until I heard a loud "SON!" I turned to see the captain and quartermaster leading three frogdogs my way.

"Son what a suprise, we could use your help wrangling these guys, you'll be in charge of cleaning up after them so may as well get used to em." The three of us led the  Frogdogs down to the harbor, and on the ship. That night forward we ate so many eggs we got sick of em, for me that bread would always an exception though. Next morning The Captain took a log barge headed to the Leagues of The Wooded Coves regional Guild Hall to cash a promissary note from the Prince-Bishopric of Jaysos for the supplies we brought. I was told to help the Navigator organize in addition to my cleaning duties. The Navigator a quiet old elf with messy long grey hair and matching beard, wearing a chaperon and robes covered in stars showed me how to read maps, take measurements using different navigational tools. 

Organizing his maps I noticed a bunch of them were missing details that could cause a shipwreck, specifically a coral reef off the coast, that had some divers and fishermen, but if a large merchant ship like ours tried to go through, well the divers might have something new to dive for. The old elf chuckled, and brought out a set of dusty tools from under his bed, and showed me how to add those hazards to the map saying, "This was a cheap map anyways so even if you mess it up we needed a new one anyways" he showed me how to measure where it was to get it on the map as accurately as possible, and showed me the best shorthand for it, we did the same for some breaks and islets.

We'd been working on improving the map till the sun went down, completely forgetting about the organizing, the old elf telling me normally id be too old to be taken on as an apprentice but he'd make sure I'm taken as an exception saying I was a natural and had "an eye for the practical aspects of cartography most navigators don't," he said I'd probably still have to clean and do whatever the captain needed of me but I'd be able to keep learning about navigation. We kept working on improvements to the map, the elf as energized as I'd ever seen him usually he just stood by the captain with a peaceful but stern look now he was grinning, when we heard cheers from outside. We rushed to join the crowd outside and see what all the excitement was about.

The crowd surrounded the gangplank as the quartermaster crossed with a big bag and started handing out Purpleheart wood coins 2 to everyone except the Officers, who I suppose had some other arrangement. The captain followed the quartermaster giving a speech about how we did this as a crew, and there was more where that came from. A celebration followed long into the night, the quartermaster opened a barrel of ale he got last night and we all ate fresh fish from the bay. 

The next morning The Captain okayed me spending the day helping the navigator get ready to take off that afternoon, the captain himself making deals in the hills just outside the city. The Navigator and some crewmembers showed me the ropes literally around the ship, and how to operate some of the  steering equipment, as we made preparations to set off. Once the Captain got back we set sail, he told us instead of Cyodue though, we'd be making for the Paldriean colonies.

Once we renavigated towards our new destination, The Navigator began showing me constellations, and how to use the stars to check our position. For the next couple weeks we traveled, stopping once at a small dock to resupply, we began to get into a routine, over the course of the day I would clean the ship and do whatever the captain and crew asked of me, at night the navigator would teach me about the stars, after a couple weeks of which we began to see land, the next day we went into a long straight and into a bay city.

We docked far from the main port in a dingy shantytown hugging the walls of the city. The Captain said not to go to far, we'd be loading up and heading right back out. I stayed on the ship, and made a rough map of the bay, until I heard the captain shout for us to get ready to load, a bunch of masked men dropping sacks from carts they pulled over unto the dock which we loaded onto the ship, noting the smell making some of us kind of hazy must be why they had the masks. Once we were all loaded the masked men had already disappeared and the captain rushed us out of the bay, I could see a handful of chainmail clad soldiers on the dock we left as we went back into that straight.

After another weeks long trip of cleaning, hardtack with frogdog eggs and jerky, look at stars repeat, I was happy to see my home city, even if we weren't stopping there yet. we passed by Septea till we were back on open water for a few more days with no stops since we didn't stop at Cyodue we were running low on supplies. it was about noon when I saw a landmass I was confused looking at the map, when I asked my mentor he chuckled and handed me a looking glass. I looked through and all I saw were ships some docked together further away but all centered around many almost piled up together, where its hard to tell if they're ancient shipwrecks or they're trapped by the ships surrounding them if it even matters at this point. As I stared in disbelief a voice came from behind me, "Son, that there is Sargosy largest floating bazaar I know of, and a city of the most salty, cantankerous, barnacles of people I've ever met," I handed the glass back to The Navigator.

The Navigator told me more about these sea markets, how they float around Inundatia, druids using spells to control winds and currents so they don't run aground but otherwise going where the waves take them making them hard to map. I asked if it could be predicted where they'd go he said that he didn't know but some knowledgeable druids, sages, or any wisemen in the city might, but they don't like to tell Septeans on account of the strictness of the guilds and churches authorities. 

The Navigator also told me a bit of this particular sea markets history, the central part was a massive ship graveyard from pre flood into a sort of mass of smashed together ghost ships, eventually three different crews of vagabond scavengers came to blows over potential loot in the ghost ships. A half orc half goliath named pouty frown eye due to his short tusks not showing above his bottom lip but shoving it into a permanent pout and his crew the pouters under the flag of the dour skull. The Dread Pirate Timmy at the time quite old but captaining a crew of some sort since the age of 11 starting with a tiny sailboat his father had for fishing and using it to smuggle ill gotten goods out of the city for a hefty fee with his friends, his crew then and now call themselves the dreaded delinquents under the flag of many skulls. The Third crew was The Mighty Fiendish crew of Harold Hairyhands a Tiefling with Gorilla like arms, his crew of primarily tieflings, disgraced devils and demons flew under the Flag of the horned skull and hairy arms. 

Nobody knows what kept the crews from coming to blows, or what they saw in those ghost ships but it made them all powerful enough to erect massive pirate fleets and using the wrecks as a headquarters build a massive bounty of plunder and loot, until Timmy had the idea to fence the goods straight from their HQ, eventually becoming the Saragosy we see today. The Captain told us since we got here early we'd anchor a ways away from the actual city to not get trapped, and wait while some of us could take the rowboats over. The Navigator suggested we inquire about the movements of the Sea Market, so we took a rowboat over there, one crewman ferrying people over in groups of six. We walked a ways through the mismatch of visiting trapped and intentionally permanent tradeships around us when my stomach began to rumble as we pass a massive tree on leaning on some other boats, what would've been its roots in the air

The Navigator stopped me standing outside, he made some motions as little specs of glowing light in front of him made shifting constellations until he said, "alright just sent a message to a friend that we  would meet him hear, he said he'd be here soon but we should grab a seat." We went in to a relaxed tavern energy, some tables getting boisterous but this was awhile after lunch so relatively quiet. We sat in a booth, and looked at a menu on the wall while we waited. Appearently this restaurant served Driftwood Gnome, which I knew were pests but was a little too close to Halfling for me, luckily they had some fish and seaweed dishes. 

We had just ordered when a man, with a nearly a whole reef on his back, antlers of coral, and a mask with a something coming out one eye, but it was hard to see beneath the hood. The man pulled a chair up and sat, saying "So old friend, you call upon us for aid?" while sort of looking past The Navigator, who in turn responded as our food got here and I dug in. "Well my oceanic peer, my young apprentice here is learning the ways of Navigation, and has had some interesting questions," This fish was actually quite good, it had mushrooms that absorbed the citrusy fish with the seaweed really well, so I hadn't been paying much attention when I was brought up, "He wanted to learn about the currents, and how they affect Saragosy." I swallowed a bite of mushroom and fish, "Uh yeah, I just realized if theirs multiple places like this why aren't they on maps, like predictions of their movements, like roughly even" I think the druid laughed but he didn't move just staring between me and The Navigator, who seemed to just accept it.

"That would be a monumental task wouldn't it, you'd need much more information than I can provide, but I'll give you that of the deep." The thing in his eye moved, and I saw it was a sea anemone it wanted to enter my mind, it already had so that it could speak to me telepathically without realizing it, but now it wanted to truly fill it. I looked to The Navigator who nodded, and I permitted the Anemone into my mind, where it showed me the deep sea, volcanoes deep beneath the ocean and how the hot air from them affected currents. The information was overwhelming and I almost fell into my food, but gathered myself and dazedly thanked the reefman. Who nodded and wordleslly stood up and left, The Navigator and I finished eating and did the same after paying.

We walked around Saragosy some more, stopping into a store on a small rowed ship that goes between some of the further apart ships in the middle areas, it smelled strongly of incense and was full of all sorts of strange stones and materials, The Navigator filled a big with ones he needed for "divining the stars that our path may be true" paid the shopkeeper and left. After walking further, into the city near the pile of wrecks that make up the core The Navigator told me, "We're gonna visit another of my old friends, I think you've proven it'd be useful for you to have your own tools to make maps, and this place has quite the craftsmen." We entered a Bazaar centered around a massive metalic ship with tanks of liquid covering it, and masts with no rigging. Ships in the bazzar got higher up as they got to tanker and scaffolding between them covered lower ones.

We were near the top deck of the tanker when from a gangplank one of its lower decks the Navigator and I ran into The Quartermaster, and The Cook. They said they had just ordered water delivered to the ship from the Gnomish Water Barons on the tanker, The Navigator told them were they were going and wanted to come along, The Cook wanting some new knives, and the quartermaster interested in improving the crews weaponry. We took a steep ramp down to the lower decks until we reached a small boat attatched to a larger dome completely unlit and under the shade of at least 3 layers of scaffold. We walked onto the small sloop which had a handful of metal diver suits strewn around that the officers were getting into and the navigator insisted I did too.

We went into a room and attached our suits to hoses, when The Quartermaster pressed a button water began to fill the room when I panicked everyone laughed and insisted this is supposed to happen the suits will provide us air, and keep us dry. I was still nervous but my panic receeded as the room filled and they were right. We opened the door on the other side of the room, and we entered an incredibly dim but a light glow from our helmets allowing us to see, smaller domes and various aquatic life filled the area, most notable among them some sort of glowing shrimp on two legs some moving things from dome to dome, some just chatting, a couple seemingly guarding shops, I awed in marvel when I noticed the Cook and Quartermaster had already gone off, and the Navigator dragging me in a rush to keep up.

We entered, a building with a lot of shelves of different metallic tools, weapons, and various equipment all of goldish almost see through material, like it was made out of the sun hitting water. The Navigator showed me a set of beautiful Cartography tools with sheets of waterproof paper, when I asked how much it would be he insisted it was his treat, to make sure I got a good start on my own cartography. We checked out with the cook, who got a new knife set, while the the quartermaster negotiated with one of the shrimp on a shipment of cutlasses.

I was trailing behind the Navigator and the Cook as the Navigator told her about the food we had, she turned to me and said "Well laddy, if ye like fish ye should come pick out fish for tonight's dinner with me," We split up once we got to the ferry back, the navigator going back on the boat while me and the Cook  checked out the fish markets along the outer ships, the Cook asking all sorts of questions about them way over my head and often there's, about flavor profiles, pairings, best methods to cook them in mass, eventually settling on some prawns just as the Quartermaster arrived to barter for them.

Once The Quartermaster got the price as low as he could and the fishers to ship all the fish we'd need onto our boat, we took their dinghy back to our boat. The Cook excitedly told us about this amazing prawn dish she had planned, but the whole time I could only think about the shrimp people we met earlier today, what would this be like to them, would it be like if we saw them eating monkeys, or like some other mammal. As I was debating all this in my head we got up to the ship and back on.

I had to do ferry duty till dinner which mostly consisted of me sitting by the docks examining my new cartographers tools, until a couple groups began getting back for dinner, including the captain with a huge bag of money he'd appearently won gambling. We ferried them back till everyone was back on the ship, the cook had made a celebratory dinner of the shrimp. 

After Dinner, I sat in the Navigators quarters trying out my new cartographers tools till I fell asleep at the desk, making copies of his world maps with what I'd learned about currents on it. We woke up to a loud horn, and shouts about preparing cargo, I immediately got up hazily lifting the bags we got in Paldriea into a ship with similarly shady looking guys, who made me wonder how shady we looked, how shady were we? I didn't think about it as much when two of the people from the other crew brought over a big bag with the distinctive clink of wood coins.

The Captain sent us back to bed till daylight came and we'd set out so I tried to go back and catch up on sleep, till light started to come to the lower decks along preceded by shouting. "where did all these people come from?" "We're boxed in, we'll never leave Saragosy" I got up and headed to the top deck to see the commotion. there was only one to the side and a couple in front and behind, but the entire space between us and the center of Saragosy had all filled up.

The Captain was arguing with the captains of the neighboring ships their crews mostly gone ashore appearently. I ran into, the Quartermaster who had a pretty glum view of things. When I got an idea, I asked if I could borrow the Quartermasters rigging knife he drunkenly handed it to me swigging a bottle of rum, "looks like ill never get to use this damn thing, got it pitched in with the cutlasses comin in," I unsheathed it seeing it was one of those prawn ones. I promised the Quartermaster he'd get to use it, if he could just get the ship moving when the time came.

I climbed the rigging at the front mast, and swung to the ship in the front, cutting the anchor, then as much of the rigging such that the sails would fall, before swinging to the ship to ours is side, the crew left on the first ship panicking. On the second ship I cut the rigging so the sails would fall lopsided, turning it slightly, until I cut the anchor sending it in the direction of the first, before I could swing back over a big bruiser of a man grabbed me and began questioning me as I tried to point out the immenent crash we would both be in. He reeled back to punch me in the face when the ships crashed, his hand still gripping the wrist of the hand I had the rigging knife in slipped and hit him in the eye, he pulled it out as we hit the water, grabbing me by the shirt and slashing my cheek with it before being pulled apart by the waves.

I floated in the water, unable to move and to this day I dont really know why. I didn't sink just floated, as the mix of salt water and blood sat in my mouth with the rigging knife. Eventually the waves lapping at me got the better of me and everything went dark, as my pantleg unhooked from the ships wreckage and I felt something grab under my armpits.

I woke up with my cheek stitched the rigging knife sheathed beside me, and the ship surgeon looking at me
